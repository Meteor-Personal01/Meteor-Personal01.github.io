INTRODUCTION
A boy who lives with his father is growing up. Now in fifth grade, he is ready to start a new chapter of his life. However, he begins to notice differences between his family and everyone else’s. Having lost his mother at a young age, he feels isolated and different from those around him. With the support of his community, he gradually learns to find his place and begins to thrive. An emotional story about the trials of growing up.
